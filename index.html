Publishing & platform standards

Responsible app development and publishing.

When an app is prepared for Google Play or the Apple App Store, we review the applicable developer requirements and the app's actual features before submission.

Privacy & data transparency

Apps should explain what personal data is collected, why it is used, how it is shared, how long it is retained and how users can contact the developer. Privacy disclosures must match actual app behaviour and third-party SDKs.

User consent & permissions

Request only permissions needed for a clear feature. Explain sensitive access in context and obtain consent where required. Do not collect or use data deceptively.

Account & data deletion

If an app allows users to create accounts, provide the account and data deletion options required by the relevant store policies, including an appropriate web deletion route where required.

Payments & subscriptions

Digital goods, subscriptions and in-app features must use the payment method required by the applicable store rules, subject to regional exceptions and current program terms. Clearly disclose price, renewal, trial and cancellation terms.

Content, safety & intellectual property

Apps should not contain prohibited, deceptive, harmful or infringing content. We aim to use properly licensed code, fonts, images, audio and other assets, and provide user reporting or moderation features where relevant.

Quality, testing & store listing

Before submission, check app stability, functionality, age/content ratings, accurate screenshots and descriptions, support details and all required declarations in the relevant developer console.

Important: This section describes our development principles; it is not a claim of Google or Apple approval, certification, partnership or guaranteed compliance. Store policies change and each app must be reviewed against the current rules for its category, features and regions before submission.
