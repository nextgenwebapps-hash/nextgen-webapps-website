RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Built with care for users and platforms.

Every app is different. Requirements are reviewed against the actual features, data practices and regions in which it will be offered.

01

Privacy & transparency

Explain what information an app collects, why it is used, and how users can exercise their privacy choices.

02

Permissions & consent

Request only permissions needed for the feature and provide clear, contextual explanations.

03

Account & data controls

Where accounts are supported, provide the account and data controls required by applicable rules.

04

Payments & subscriptions

Present pricing and renewal terms clearly and follow the payment rules applicable to each platform and region.

05

Content & intellectual property

Use content and assets responsibly, with appropriate rights and safeguards for the product's features.

06

Testing & store information

Review stability, functionality, age ratings, store descriptions, screenshots and required declarations.

These are development principles, not a claim of Google or Apple approval, certification, partnership or guaranteed compliance. Store policies can change; each release must be assessed against current requirements.
