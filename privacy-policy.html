This website does not currently provide a visitor account or an on-page contact form. Information sent to us may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested project information, maintain website security, and meet legal obligations. We do not state that information is collected for advertising or profiling through this website.

The current website is designed without advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting infrastructure or embedded third-party services may use technical storage or logs. Update this section if you add analytics, embedded content, tracking pixels or other cookie-based tools.

We retain enquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage business records and meet applicable legal requirements. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Email NextGen.webapps@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”.
