Privacy Policy.

How this website handles information and how you can contact us about privacy.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the privacy practices for the NEXTGEN WEB&APPS public website. It does not replace a separate privacy policy for any mobile application or digital product. Each app must provide disclosures that accurately describe its own data practices.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name and message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and maintain relevant business correspondence.

Website technical information

When this website is hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested page and request time in server or security logs. The exact retention period and processing configuration depend on the hosting provider and its settings.

Cookies and analytics

This website is not intentionally configured to use advertising cookies or third-party analytics in its current version. Hosting or security infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms. This statement should be reviewed if additional analytics, embeds or marketing tools are introduced.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by email and website infrastructure providers as needed to deliver the service, or disclosed where legally required.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable care, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have sent us, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

Children

This business website is not directed to children and does not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, email NextGen.webapps@gmail.com or write to the business address on our Contact page.

Before publishing, verify hosting provider log settings, retention periods, email services and any third-party scripts against the live configuration.
